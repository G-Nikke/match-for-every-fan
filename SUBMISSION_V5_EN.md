# Match for Every Fan — Demo V5

Match for Every Fan helps viewers follow football footage through concise scene descriptions, energetic live calls, and an on-demand recap. It is designed around accessibility: the viewer chooses between visual context and broadcast rhythm, with optional speech and simple tone controls.

Microsoft Foundry powers image interpretation and the recap. During AI Mode, a sampled frame is sent to a configured image-capable deployment. The model returns a cautious caption and visible shape estimates. When the viewer presses **Summarize this segment**, the app sends prior AI commentary lines to the model and displays a short summary. The demo uses a credited recorded sample or footage the viewer selects. It does not claim an official live match feed or full coverage between samples.
